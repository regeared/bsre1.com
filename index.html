What we offer

BSRE Brawl Stars Mod and CRRE tools

Regeared offers BSRE for Brawl Stars Mod features like Autododge, Aimbot, Aim Assist, Xray, and Killaura, plus CRRE tools including Card Counter, Elixir Tracker, Cycle Shower, Evo Tracker, and placement markers.

CRRE

  • Elixir Counter: Displays the exact amount of Elixir held by the opponent.
  • Cycle Viewer: Displays the opponent's current cycle.
  • Evo Tracker: Tracks Evo cycles of the opponent's evos.
  • Last Used Deck: Shows the last deck played by the opponent.
  • Location Marker: Shows the tile where Miner, Goblin Barrel, or Goblin Drill is deployed.
  • Spell Lethal Highlight: Highlights enemies when the selected spell takes them down.
  • Troop Range Viewer: Shows troop range while dragging for precise placement.
  • Spectator Support: Use basic features while spectating from another account or device.

BSRE

  • Auto Dodge: Built for fast automatic dodging.
  • Aim Bot and Aim Assist: Helps hit enemies without manual aiming.
  • Xray: Helps target enemies even in bushes.
  • Killaura: Automatically handles close-range combat.
  • Hide Gadget: Prevents enemies from seeing gadget usage.
  • Hold2Shoot: Shoot continuously without lifting your finger.
  • Show Ammo: Displays enemy ammo bars.
  • AutoFarm: Supports low-trophy farming.

CRRE+

  • Includes all CRRE features.
  • Ghost Drop: Shows enemy placements up to 0.7 seconds before they happen for normal cards and spells.
